## Intent

A promo that sells Naari Kavach, a free safety web app for women in Maharashtra.
The user's own words: "Show the best sides and use cases, along with good
storyline and awareness."

Concept confirmed by the user, "One evening": one woman's evening in Mumbai in
five moments.

1. The fact (hook): in Maharashtra about 1 in 4 women aged 18 to 49 report
   physical or sexual violence, and 2% of them go to the police. Most safety
   apps then ask her to do more.
2. Before she leaves: routes ranked by risk, the hardest part named, and a map
   that fades where it is only guessing.
3. On the way: the quiet shield. Phone in her bag, no check-ins, walking on
   answers the one question it asks.
4. Something feels off: one tap to watch closely, three presses on the earphone
   button, hold for SOS and 112.
5. If she does not arrive, and after: the sealed trail opens to the people she
   chose; the rights guide and the ward complaint that gets the street fixed.
6. Close: "Track risk, never people." Questions she was asked tonight: 0.

Tone: calm and steady, never frightening. The product's own rule is "calm under
stress": no alarming imagery, red only for SOS.
